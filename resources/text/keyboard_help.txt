TYPING
Tap a key, then tap again to cycle its letters. Pause, or tap another key, to keep the letter.

NUMBERS
Hold any key for its number. On the 123 page, hold 0 for a space.

SHIFT (bottom-left)
Tap once for a capital, again for CAPS LOCK. Hold Shift to switch the layout. On the 123 and #+= pages, tapping it switches the layout.

SPECIAL LETTERS
Letters like a and o cycle to a A a A a. Turn them on or off in Settings.

BUTTONS
Up: settings
OK: enter (hold for a new line)
Down: switch layout (hold to delete)
Back: go back
